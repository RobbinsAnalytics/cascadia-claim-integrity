Cascadia Claim Integrity / Report Sprawl

The reports multiplied.
Which ones still matter?

What exists, what overlaps, and what can safely go?

Reports multiply. Ownership, definitions and dependencies become harder to trace. Clearing the estate starts with decisions about what people rely on, not a deletion list.

  • Before a change: Establish purpose, ownership and evidence.
  • Before new work: Check reuse and the prerequisites for the intended decision.

01 / Inventory

What exists, and what is still unknown?

Start with an asset and its intended use. The reporting analyst records owner, consumers, sensitivity, definitions, cadence and dependencies before proposing change.

Accountable

Business owner

The business owner confirms purpose and consumers. The analyst checks all use routes, including scheduled deliveries and periodic obligations.

Done when

An inventory with explicit evidence gaps. Inactivity is never inferred from an incomplete log.

03 / Retirement

What can safely go?

A candidate for retirement is not permission to delete. Protect the consumer before withdrawing the route.

Accountable

Business owner

The business owner approves the bounded change. The analyst verifies replacements and dependencies, records consumer notice, retention expectations, an observation window covering actual cadence and a rollback route.

Done when

A recoverable transition or a documented hold. Silence and low usage are not approval.

Follow a worked case
What evidence belongs beside the decision?

Validate, authorize, notify, then observe. The business owner approves the bounded change. The analyst verifies replacements and dependencies, records consumer notice, retention expectations, an observation window covering actual cadence and a rollback route. Keep the source, observed result, accountable role and next action together. If the check is incomplete, retain the gap rather than infer clearance.

04 / Access

Start with purpose, audience and fields. A restricted contributor may need structural metadata rather than sensitive source rows.

05 / Prevention

What keeps the estate from refilling?

Give requests a front door. Start from a business question and search for reuse before commissioning another report.

Accountable

Reporting sponsor

The sponsor and metric steward agree definition, population, timing, source lineage, quality, access and acceptance evidence. Assign owner, steward and review triggers.

Done when

A maintained asset or a bounded readiness hold, rather than another unsupported report.

Follow a worked case
What evidence belongs beside the decision?

Gate the use, then maintain the asset. The sponsor and metric steward agree definition, population, timing, source lineage, quality, access and acceptance evidence. Assign owner, steward and review triggers. Keep the source, observed result, accountable role and next action together. If the check is incomplete, retain the gap rather than infer clearance.
